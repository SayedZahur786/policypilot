# Privacy policy

Effective date: 21 November 2026

## Who operates this service
PolicyPilot is an early preview project maintained by Sayed Zahur. Contact the maintainer through the GitHub profile at https://github.com/SayedZahur786. Do not post private recovery keys or personal data publicly. A dedicated private support and legal contact must be configured before paid launch.

## Information and purpose
The public preview provides an illustrative policy set. When database-backed intake is enabled, we store your app details, operator name, business address, contact email, answers and generated revisions to provide and maintain your policies. Published documents make those supplied business details public. Do not supply sensitive personal information.

## Cookies and analytics
We use an essential HttpOnly management session cookie lasting seven days. A recovery key is shown once and temporarily retained in browser session storage until you hide it or close the tab. We do not run analytics, advertising cookies or cross-site tracking. The shared analytics component remains disabled.

## Service providers and transfers
The application is designed for Vercel hosting and Neon Postgres storage. Stripe handles checkout when payment credentials are configured; PolicyPilot does not collect full card numbers. These providers may process information in other countries under their own agreements. In local development only, records are stored in a private local file instead of Neon. Public preview does not accept purchases while payment setup is incomplete.

## Retention and security
Policy drafts and published revisions remain stored until the operator removes them. This preview has no automatic retention job. Request removal through the maintainer contact; private support must be established before accepting live customer records. We store a hash of management keys, use server-side validation and restrict editing to possession of a valid key. No system can guarantee complete security.

## Your choices and rights
You may request access, correction or deletion by contacting the maintainer. Applicable regional rights depend on your circumstances and governing law. Editing access requires your recovery key; keep it secure and do not share it. You can clear the essential session cookie using browser controls. Do not enter real customer data into a preview that has not completed its release review.

## Changes
We publish changes on this page with an updated effective date. Material changes to enabled services or data practices will be reflected here before those services accept live customer information.
