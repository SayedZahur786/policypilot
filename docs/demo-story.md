# PolicyPilot walkthrough
1. Hook: your app is ready; the fine print is the loose end.
2. Show the real landing page and its three-policy sample.
3. Complete ten questions about an illustrative adult SaaS and its data practices.
4. Review generated privacy, terms and refund drafts; show the HTML export.
5. Clearly label local payment simulation; no actual charge is made.
6. Open the hosted policies, then change the app name and republish at the same URL.
7. Close with the distinction: working draft workflow, legal review still required.

Production: real Playwright recording, system text-to-speech narration, large burned-in captions. Under 90 seconds. Recording must hide the management key before any frame is shared.
