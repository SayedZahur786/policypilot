# Clause research and limits

Reviewed 21 November 2026. Original clause text is a concise draft for the bounded intake; sources inform its coverage but do not certify it.

- EU GDPR Articles 13–22: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/TXT/?uri=CELEX%3A32016R0679 . Transparency requires controller identity, purposes, legal basis, recipients, transfers, retention and rights; the intake groups these subjects, and incomplete operator-specific requirements still need review.
- India commencement notification: https://www.meity.gov.in/static/uploads/2025/11/c56ceae6c383460ca69577428d36828b.pdf . The November 2025 notification phases commencement; core provisions are scheduled eighteen months later. The India draft does not claim all rights were in force in November 2026.
- India rules: https://www.meity.gov.in/documents/act-and-policies/digital-personal-data-protection-rules-2025-gDOxUjMtQWa . Check current implementation and commencement with qualified counsel.

US text is deliberately conditional on applicable state laws and thresholds. The ten-question model cannot establish CCPA applicability or enumerate every state obligation. EU representatives, DPO requirements, transfer mechanisms, legitimate-interest balancing, exact analytics technologies and product-specific consumer rights require additional professional review. The generator excludes child-directed and sensitive-data products. Do not equate region selection with compliance.
